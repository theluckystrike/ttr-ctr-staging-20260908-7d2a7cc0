Calculation method

Search Console defines CTR as clicks divided by impressions. Google's CTR definition

CTR is clicks divided by impressions. Google's definition

Percentage-point change
(later CTR fraction − earlier CTR fraction) × 100
Relative CTR change
(later CTR fraction − earlier CTR fraction) / earlier CTR fraction × 100
Net click change
later clicks − earlier clicks
Impression component
(later impressions − earlier impressions) × (earlier CTR fraction + later CTR fraction) / 2
CTR component
(later CTR fraction − earlier CTR fraction) × (earlier impressions + later impressions) / 2

Read the components as signed click equivalents. Use their sum to check the net click change. Don't interpret the split as causal attribution, statistical significance or an A/B-test verdict.

Calculations use unrounded ratios; displayed results are rounded. Rounded component totals can differ slightly from the raw click change. Calculator source. When a period has no impressions, leave its CTR and both components undefined. Keep the raw click change. When earlier CTR is zero, leave relative CTR change undefined.
